# Career questions

## Could AI reduce jobs?

It can reduce human effort for some tasks, and organizations may change staffing or hiring. A successful code-generation example does not establish that an entire developer role can be replaced. Tasks, responsibilities and employment outcomes are different things.

## Which work is changing?

Clearly specified, repetitive implementation can be easier to delegate. Examples include familiar scaffolding, similar conversions and routine test drafts. Requirements, integration, review and accountability still need attention, and AI can assist in those areas too. This is not a list of safe or doomed job titles; experienced developers and testers also face changing expectations.

## What does the evidence say?

A Stanford U.S. payroll study, revised in August 2026, reports weaker young-worker employment in AI-exposed occupations, largely through hiring. The authors frame the findings as descriptive, not causal. It does not predict an individual developer's outcome or establish effects in every country. [Study and limitations](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/canaries-in-the-coal-mine-six-facts-about-the-recent-employment-effects-of-artificial-intelligence/)

## What can I do?

Keep engineering fundamentals active; learn to provide useful goals and context; inspect changes and relevant checks; strengthen knowledge of your domain; and retain evidence of outcomes you understand. These are practical preparation priorities, not a guarantee of employment.
